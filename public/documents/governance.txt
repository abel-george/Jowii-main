Consolidated Institutional Structure for the JOWIH Organization

I. STEWARDSHIP & FOUNDER-ERA ADVISORY STRUCTURE

1. Founder & Steward President

The Founder & Steward President serves as the principal constitutional steward of the JOWIH Mission, exercising the strategic, protective, and reserved powers established by the Constitution.

2. Founder’s Council - Founder Era

The Founder’s Council is the Founder-era advisory structure chaired by the Founder & Steward President.

It consists of the Founder and persons invited by the Founder like special advisers to provide strategic counsel.

The Council is not a separate operating or executive authority and does not exercise independent management powers.

3. Special Advisors to the Steward President

Special Advisors are individual appointments serving directly at the discretion of the Steward President. They may participate in the Founder’s Council when invited by the Founder.

The initial advisory portfolio may include:

Special Advisor -- Development & Infrastructure

Special Advisor -- Strategic Partnerships & Acquisitions

Special Advisor -- Finance & Jowih Credits 

Special Advisor -- Technology & Innovation

Special Advisor -- Legal & Compliance

Special Advisor -- Diplomatic & Public Relations

Special Advisor -- Economics

Additional Special Advisors as required.

4. Foundation Advisory Council — Successor Era

Upon transition from the Founder era, the Founder’s Council shall transition into the Foundation Advisory Council in accordance with the Constitution.

The Foundation Advisory Council shall serve as the successor Steward President's advisory structure.

The Founder’s Council and Foundation Advisory Council are therefore successive forms of the same constitutional advisory architecture and shall not operate concurrently as separate councils.

II. JOWIH FOUNDATION GOVERNANCE

5. Board of Governors

The Board of Governors constitutes the principal governing body of the Jowih Foundation and exercises the governance, fiduciary, oversight, and strategic responsibilities assigned to it by the Constitution.

6. Permanent Board Committees

XIII. CORE OPERATING SUBSIDIARIES

The Group shall establish and operate a limited number of Core Operating Subsidiaries corresponding to the actual corporate businesses approved under the Group structure.

Operating principle

One Core Operating Subsidiary - One CEO - Multiple related businesses, divisions, and operating units.

The existence of a particular GAG project, technology, product, or service does not automatically create a separate subsidiary or CEO position.

Accordingly:

CRT does not automatically require a separate CRT CEO;

robotaxis do not automatically require a separate Robotaxi CEO;

robovans do not automatically require a separate Robovan CEO;

individual R&D Centers do not require separate CEOs;

individual products or platforms do not automatically constitute subsidiaries.

Where appropriate, such activities shall operate as divisions, business units, projects, or subsidiaries under the relevant Core Operating Company.

52. The Fund as a Distinct Governance Domain

The Jowih Master Ecosystem Fund ("Master Fund") is a Foundation-established investment vehicle structured as a Mauritius Variable Capital Company (VCC).

The Master Fund:

is established by the Jowih Foundation;

is a separate legal entity from Jowih Holdings Limited;

is not under the operational control of Jowih Holdings Limited;

operates under its own governance framework;

provides regulated economic participation to permitted investors; and

does not exercise governance over the Jowih Foundation, Jowih Holdings, Jowih Group, or the Operating Companies.

The Master Fund is a distinct governance domain within the JOWIH institutional architecture.

58. Stewardship and Voting

58.1 The Fund as Legal Owner of Vaulted Shares

The Fund, through its applicable VCC sub-fund compartments, is the legal owner of the vaulted shares deposited by partners.

As legal owner, the Fund holds the voting rights attached to those shares.

Unit holders have economic exposure to the Fund's NAV and distributable performance. Unit holders do not have direct voting rights over the vaulted shares. Unit holders do not have governance rights over the underlying companies or over the Jowih Foundation.

This structure preserves the absolute decoupling of downstream economic rights from upstream control rights.
