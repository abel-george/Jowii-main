The Jowih Master Ecosystem Fund ("Master Fund") is the definitive regulated economic-participation and asset-capital layer of the Jowih commercial ecosystem.

The Master Fund is designed to aggregate qualifying commercial assets, establish independently determined valuations, issue regulated non-voting investment units, and provide permitted investors with economic exposure to defined Fund portfolios without providing governance access to the Jowih Foundation, its constitutional mission, or its protected governance architecture.

The architecture establishes a permanent separation between:

mission governance;

commercial ownership and strategic control;

operational execution; and

regulated economic participation.

30% Jowih Core / 70% Ecosystem Partners

Asset Participation:Eligible assets are vaulted into the Fund and represented through regulated economic participation.

Development Settlement:Approved projects may use Fund Units as part of legally permissible consideration for qualifying land transactions.

Neither mechanism transfers governance of the Jowih Foundation to Fund participants.

XXX. KEY STRUCTURAL DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this Blueprint:

"Master Fund"

Means the proposed Jowih Master Ecosystem Fund and its applicable compartments, cells, Unit classes and associated investment architecture.

"Jowih Core"

Means qualifying Jowih Group companies and qualifying Jowih-origin commercial enterprises admitted under the applicable eligibility framework.

"Ecosystem Partner"

Means a qualifying third-party enterprise admitted through the Partner Registry or another approved ecosystem admission mechanism.

"Jowih 100"

Means the initial qualifying enterprise universe of up to 100 eligible commercial enterprises.

"Jowih 500"

Means the expansion capacity toward a maximum target universe of 500 qualifying enterprises under the proposed Index architecture, subject to future legal and structural amendment where required.

"Neutral Reserve"

Means the separately administered category for qualifying assets outside the 30% Jowih Core / 70% Ecosystem Partner commercial allocation. It is a percentage backing, not an outstanding unit pool. It adds to NAV. It backs every unit. It causes appreciation, not dilution.

"Master Ecosystem Unit"

Means a permitted non-voting investment unit representing defined economic participation in the applicable Fund or Unit class.

"Treasury Capacity"

Means authorized but unissued Fund-unit capacity available for future permitted issuance.

"Authorized Capacity"

Means the total unit capacity legally and structurally authorized under the applicable Fund documentation.

"Outstanding Units"

Means Units actually issued and held by investors or other entitled recipients.

"Appreciation"

Means the increase in NAV per unit that occurs when NAV grows but units outstanding remains the same. Appreciation is the mechanism through which unit holders benefit from company success and land appreciation.

"Forward Pricing"

Means the mechanism through which new units are created at the current NAV per unit when new capital is raised or neutral assets are admitted. New Units Created = New Cash Invested or Value of Neutral Asset ÷ Current NAV per Unit. No dilution in value. Just new capital or new asset backing.

"Land Appreciation"

Means the increase in land value that occurs when development happens around the land. Land in the Neutral Reserve appreciates when the Foundation drives development across the continent. The appreciation flows to NAV per unit.

"Asset Lock"

Means the structural condition in which vaulted shares are held within the applicable VCC sub-fund compartment without carrying governance representation through the Fund. The Fund does not vote the shares. The partner may repurchase them subject to lock-up and Fund terms.

"ADFA"

Means the Accelerated Development Framework Agreement — the regulatory framework established by the Jowih Foundation and participating governments governing cross-border operations, land settlement, and regulatory recognition in host jurisdictions.

"Land Acquisition & Settlement Facility"

Means the proposed mechanism through which qualifying projects may, where legally permissible, use Master Fund Units alongside cash as consideration for qualifying land transactions.

"NAV per Unit"

Means Total Net Asset Value divided by Units Outstanding. Determines the economic value of each unit at any given time. Increases when NAV grows and units outstanding remains the same.

"Unit Price"

Means the price at which units are issued or redeemed. At issuance, it is determined by the Fund based on NAV and accessibility objectives. On secondary market, it is determined by market supply and demand.
