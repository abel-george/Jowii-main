What jowih is

Jowih is a development organization - an infrastructure and economic coordination initiative designed to enable sovereign African nations to accelerate development, trade more freely across borders, and develop more rapidly, without surrendering control over their own affairs. It is not political. Its mandate is development: infrastructure, industry, digital systems,space,services, financial rails, and continental coordination.

It operates through eleven interconnected components, including the JOWIH Organization, the Great African Grid initiative(seven corridors), Jowih Continental OS (Asta), Jowih Credits (cross border), ADFA (cooperation with 54 African states), and the Jowih Ecosystem Fund (the Jowih 100 → 500 Index).

The first-party MVP of the Asta Super App- interface to proposed wider Jowih Continental OS has already been built and is being prepared for demonstration.
