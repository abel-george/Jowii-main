THE COMPLETE GRID BOND ALLOCATION ($50-100BN)

SECTION 1: OVERVIEW

The Grid Bond is the one-time activation capital for the Great African Grid Initiative. It is raised from African citizens, the African diaspora, and global citizens through a 6-month open window. The bond is structured as two classes:

DigitalEcosystem Bond: 15-year term, 5.0-5.4% interest, first payment Year 5

Infrastructure Bond: 20-year term, 5.1-5.4% interest, first payment Year 5

Total target: $100BN
