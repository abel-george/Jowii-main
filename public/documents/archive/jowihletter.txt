Jowih — A Continental Development Initiative

What Jowih Is

Over the past two years, I have designed Jowih — a development organization and continental infrastructure and economic coordination initiative. Jowih is not a political organization. It does not seek political power or governance authority over any state. Its mandate is development: infrastructure, industry, digital systems, financial rails, human capital, and continental coordination.

Jowih is the architecture of the crossing — moving 54 African nations and the global diaspora from one continental economic state to another, in measurable milestones.

The initiative has drawn the interest of Africa Startup Festival 2026, and a partnership conversation is underway. A partnership proposal has also been submitted to the African Diaspora Network. The proposed pre-launch is approaching in September/October. Much broader view of the initiative is from its documents in the website.

The Eleven Components of Jowih

1. The JOWIH Foundation — a mission-locked institutional foundation, structured as a Company Limited by Guarantee. It cannot issue equity. It cannot be diluted. It cannot be bought. It holds the constitutional architecture, the Asset Lock, and the Golden Vote.

2. The JOWIH Purpose Trust — protecting the mission and the Foundation's 90% economic interest in the holding company.

3. The Founder Family Trust — protecting the mission from founder drift, holding a restricted golden vote against mission-threatening actions, and receiving the founder's 10% economic interest.

4. Jowih Holdings Limited — governed by representatives from both trusts, managing the Foundation's for-profit activities.

5. Jowih Group — executing and operating the commercial subsidiaries across the seven corridors.

6. The Great African Grid (GAG) — the continental development and infrastructure framework across seven corridors: Transport, Industrial & Manufacturing, Energy, Digital, Payment, Service, and Space Capability. It operates on continuous 20-year milestone Horizons.

7. Jowih Continental OS (Asta) — the continental digital infrastructure and orchestration layer enabling interoperability across the continent. The first-party MVP of the Asta Super App has already been built sample screenshots on website.

8. Jowih Credits — the settlement layer for cross-border exchange, operating through a partner-only execution model. The Foundation does not compete with partners; it provides the bridge layer.

9. ADFA — Accelerated Development Framework Agreement — the framework for structured cooperation with 54 African states, providing three execution models: State-Funded, Foundation-Funded, and Joint-Funded.

10. The Jowih Ecosystem Fund — the regulated investment vehicle for citizens and the diaspora. The Jowih 100 → 500 Index, with a fixed 30% Jowih Core / 70% Ecosystem Partner allocation.

11. Private-Public Partnership — The Pool of Companies — a pre-qualified, pre-vetted pool of private sector developers who become the Foundation's default execution partners across Africa. Three-tier execution: Continental Defining, National Strategic, and Local/CDE. Pre-qualification eliminates tendering delays. Market balance is maintained — Foundation projects are additional, not substitutional. Private sector partners retain their brands, customers, and operating models while gaining access to a larger ecosystem.

How the Economics Work

The Grid Bond is the one-time activation capital instrument. It is designed to be raised from African citizens, the diaspora, and global participants — with institutions, banks, and pension funds deliberately excluded from this phase to prevent concentrated influence at the foundation stage.

90% of designated ecosystem revenue is reinvested into continental development through the Mission Fund. Each participating country receives a yearly fiscal development budget through Country Pots — not debt, not aid. Development budgets. These funds support Community Development Enterprises and approved projects, with disbursements tied to verified milestones and accountable implementation.
